Tool

  • room — click an empty square
  • exit — click the source, then the far room, then the direction
  • pan — middle-drag; fit view re-frames the map

On the map the cardinals are n / s / e / w and the arrow points where the exit goes. Dashed links are up, down, in and out. Hover a room to read its exits in full.

Exit direction (source → far room)

Cardinal exits move a whole square. Up/down and in/out nudge the room diagonally, the way a paper map shows a cellar under its street.
